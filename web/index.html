      About the game

      How you play

      Play it face to face. Say your guesses and scores out loud to each other. This page only helps you keep track of letters. Face to face, it tells you when a word isn't in its dictionary, but never checks a score.

      Rules

      1. Each of you picks a secret word of 4 letters.
      2. Take turns guessing the other's word.
      3. The answer is how many of the guess's letters are in the word. Where they sit doesn't matter.
      4. A repeated letter counts once.
      5. First to guess the word wins, unless the other player then gets theirs on their last turn: that's a draw.

      Group game. 2 to 4 phones guess one word the computer picks, taking turns, with a clock each. Anyone in the room picks words of 4 or 5 letters, and every phone is told; it can switch at any time, even during a game for the next word. Every guess is scored for you and shows on every phone. Only dictionary words count, and never one already guessed. Once someone gets it, the players after them in the round get one last guess: more than one right is a draw. Run out of time or leave, and you're out. The room's wins count both lengths, with each one's shown.

      Example

      Word CART
      Guess TART 3

      T, A and R are in the word. The second T doesn't count again.

      Dictionary

      4 letters
      5 letters

      Every 4- and 5-letter word in British and American spelling, from the SCOWL word list. Type a word it doesn't know and it tells you. The 5-letter words are for the group game. Advanced Helper Mode counts how many your partner's word could still be.
