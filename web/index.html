    About the game

    Play it face to face. Say your guesses and scores out loud to each other. This page only helps you keep track of letters. It tells you when a word isn't in its dictionary, but never checks a score.

    Two-player clock

    A chess clock shared between your two phones. Type the same room name on both (pick one nobody else would) and connect. Noting a guess stops your clock and starts the other player's; the first guess is free. New game before the first guess restarts both clocks, and so does changing the minutes. Both phones need internet to find each other; after that they talk directly.

    Rules

    1. Each of you picks a secret word of 4 letters.
    2. Take turns guessing the other's word.
    3. The answer is how many of the guess's letters are in the word. Where they sit doesn't matter.
    4. A repeated letter counts once.
    5. First to guess the word wins.

    Example

    Word CART
    Guess TART 3

    T, A and R are in the word. The second T doesn't count again.

    Dictionary

    words it knows

    Every 4-letter word in British and American spelling, from the SCOWL word list. Type a word it doesn't know and it tells you. Advanced Helper Mode counts how many your partner's word could still be.
