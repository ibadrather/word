How to play

  1. Each of you picks a secret word of 4 letters: tap Set your word, or keep it in your head.
  2. Take turns saying a guess. The other says how many of its letters are in their word, wherever they sit.
  3. A repeated letter counts once.
  4. First to guess it wins. If the other then gets theirs on their last turn, it's a draw.
