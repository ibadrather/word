Option B · Choose the duel in the menu, settings drop down from a bar

The one you described. The header stays exactly as on main. The drawer gets a "How you play" choice at the top: Face to face (the default) or Duel clock. Choosing the duel adds a bar under the header with a dropdown. Open, it holds the connection settings. Once you're connected it folds to one line, with the clocks under it.

Trade-offs

  • + The header is untouched and the Face to face page is identical to main. The duel only exists once you choose it.
  • + One place for everything about the duel: the bar. Open, it's the settings; folded, it's the status. That replaces the status line, the menu card and the Leave button.
  • + The drawer stops being a mix of "about" and "settings": the clock card leaves the middle of it.
  • − Turning the duel on takes two steps (logo, then card), but you do that once per session.
  • − The folded bar plus the clocks take about 120px above the guess box.
