Option A · A clock button in the header

The smallest change. Main's page stays as it is, plus one round clock button next to New game. It opens a sheet that drops from the top with the duel settings. Once you're connected the button fills in and gets a green dot, and the two clocks sit under the header. The status line goes away, because the dot and the names on the clocks already tell you who's connected.

Trade-offs

  • + Main's layout is unchanged apart from one 36px button. The About drawer goes back to exactly what main has.
  • + One tap from anywhere to see or change the duel. The button's state (outlined, filled, green dot) works as the connection light.
  • − The header gets tighter on a 360px phone; "New game" already shrinks to "New" there.
  • − A clock icon is less obvious than a word. First-time players may not know it means "play against another phone".
